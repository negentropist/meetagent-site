Dimensions / Economics

Cost per completed task

What does a correct result really cost?

Why it matters

Seat prices hide retries, usage charges and review time.

How to measure it

Total the fees, usage and review time at a stated hourly rate, then divide by correct results. Compare with doing the work manually.

Main threat to the measurement

Counting every task, not just those completed correctly, makes the cost look lower than it is.

Levels

  • L1Unknown or unpredictable
  • L2Predictable, but costs more than doing it manually
  • L3About the same as manual
  • L4Clearly cheaper than manual (2× or more)
  • L5Far cheaper (10× or more), with cost reported per task
