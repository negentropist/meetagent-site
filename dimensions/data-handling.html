Why it matters

How to measure it

Main threat to the measurement

Levels

  • L1Unclear, or used for training with no opt-out
  • L2Used for training by default, with an opt-out
  • L3Not used for training by default; retention documented
  • L4Level 3 plus independent certification (SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001)
  • L5Level 4 plus admin controls and a choice of region, private deployment or self-hosting
