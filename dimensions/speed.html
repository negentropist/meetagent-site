Dimensions / Economics

Speed

How long until you have a usable result?

Why it matters

A fast answer that takes an hour to check isn't fast.

How to measure it

Time from request to a usable result, including review and correction. Report the median and the slowest cases.

Main threat to the measurement

Timing only the agent's run and ignoring the person's time.

Levels

  • L1Slower than manual once review is counted
  • L2Sometimes faster, unpredictably
  • L3About the same as manual, but predictable
  • L4Clearly faster than manual (2× or more)
  • L5Far faster (10× or more), and the slowest cases are still acceptable
