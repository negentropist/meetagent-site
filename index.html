How it works

Directories tell you what exists. MeetAgent tells you what to use.

Plain language. What you want done, who it's for, any limits like budget or tools you already use.

We match

Your task is matched against a curated list of agents we've tested ourselves, not scraped from the web.

Each with one line on why it fits, the price, and when we last tested it. Then go try the best one.

Tested, not scraped

An agent is listed only after we've used it on a real task. Every listing shows the test date.

Matched, not listed

You don't browse categories. You describe the job and get a short answer.

Honest verdicts

Where an agent falls short, we say so. A recommendation is worth nothing if everything gets five stars.
